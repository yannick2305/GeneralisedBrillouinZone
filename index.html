Lifted Generalised Brillouin Zone

Each GBZ point z is drawn at height Im a(z). Where the open limit Λ(a) is real the curve stays in the plane ℂ × {0}; over the complex arcs of Λ it lifts out of it, giving a closed curve in ℝ³.

The matrix

Tn(a) = (aj−k)j,k=1n,   ak = tR k−α,  a−k = tL k−α  (1 ≤ k ≤ m)

Real coefficients with algebraic off-diagonal decay, truncated at band width m, and a0 = 0. The matrix is non-reciprocal when tR ≠ tL. For m ≥ 2 a constant ratio tL/tR cannot be removed by a similarity diag(rj), so the open limit is free to leave the real axis.

Open limit

Order the 2m roots of zm(a(z) − λ) by modulus, |z1| ≤ … ≤ |z2m|. By Schmidt and Spitzer (1960) the eigenvalues of Tn(a) accumulate, as n → ∞, on

Λ(a) = { λ ∈ ℂ : |zm(λ)| = |zm+1(λ)| },

a finite union of analytic arcs, symmetric under λ → λ̄ because the coefficients are real. The grey loop in the λ-plane is the periodic spectrum a(S¹) for comparison.

GBZ and its lift

GBZ = closure { zm(λ), zm+1(λ) : λ ∈ Λ(a) },   a(GBZ) = Λ(a)

The map a: GBZ → Λ is two-to-one away from the arc endpoints, and both partner roots sit at the same height. The plotted curve is ι(z) = (Re z, Im z, Im a(z)). The GBZ has corners over triple points of Λ, and because a is conformal it crosses itself wherever two arcs of Λ cross. Swapping tR and tL maps the GBZ to its image under z → 1/z with the same Λ.

How it is computed

For φ ∈ (0, π], solve a(z) = a(zeiφ), a polynomial of degree 2m. A root is kept when z and zeiφ are exactly the m-th and (m+1)-th roots of zm(a(z) − λ) with λ = a(z), to relative tolerance 10−6. Roots come from Aberth–Ehrlich iteration warm-started along φ, and the φ step halves near junctions.

Checks: m = 1 gives the Hatano–Nelson circle |z| = √(tL/tR), and tL = tR gives the unit circle. For the default parameters the eigenvalues of T80, computed with 60-digit arithmetic, lie within 2·10−3 of Λ.
